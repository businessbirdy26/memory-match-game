MemoryMatch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

MemoryMatch is a memory-matching card game. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your data when you play it — on Android or in a browser as a Progressive Web App.

Your game progress stays on your device

Moves, best times, streaks, unlocked levels, stars, coins, hearts, and your settings (sound, haptics, difficulty) are all saved locally on your device using standard on-device storage. None of this is uploaded to a server we run, because we don't run one — MemoryMatch has no account system, no login, and no backend. If you uninstall the app or clear your browser's site data, that progress is gone for good; we have no copy of it to restore.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android version of MemoryMatch shows ads via Google AdMob: occasional interstitial ads between rounds or levels, and an optional rewarded video ad you can choose to watch for an extra heart. Ads never play during actual gameplay.

To serve and measure these ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect information such as your advertising ID, general device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data, and may use it to show ads that are more relevant to you. This collection is carried out by Google, not by us — we don't receive, see, or store this data ourselves. For details on what Google collects and how it's used, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's User Consent Policy.

If you'd rather not see ads at all, the app offers a one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase, processed entirely through Google Play Billing — we never see or store your payment details.

We don't sell your data

We don't collect a database of personal information in the first place, so there is nothing of yours for us to sell or trade. We don't run our own analytics or tracking beyond what's described above for ads.

Children

MemoryMatch doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If the app is configured for a child-directed audience on Google Play, ad requests are made accordingly per Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. Since this page is hosted alongside the app's source, its history is publicly visible.
